Educational infographic, clean flat design with a muted parchment-and-sepia baroque emblem-book palette, 4:3 landscape aspect ratio.

Title at top center in elegant serif capitals: "SIN (PECCATO) 죄의 도상" with small subtitle "Cesare Ripa, Iconologia".

Center of the canvas: a simplified engraving-style line drawing of a naked blindfolded young man walking on a jagged cliff path, a serpent coiled around his waist, and a small worm burrowing at his heart (heart drawn visible on his chest). Keep the figure monochrome sepia like an old etching.

Four callout panels arranged around the central figure, each connected by a thin arrow pointing to the matching body part:

1. Top-left panel, arrow to the blindfolded eyes. Icon: blindfold over eyes. Label: "BLIND YOUTH 눈멂". Caption words: "Rashness & blindness".
2. Top-right panel, arrow to the bare dark torso. Icon: a white robe slipping off, body turning dark. Label: "NAKED & BLACK 나체". Caption words: "Grace stripped away".
3. Bottom-left panel, arrow to the serpent at the waist. Icon: coiled snake wearing a small deceptive smiling mask. Label: "SERPENT 뱀". Caption words: "Devil's false good".
4. Bottom-right panel, arrow to the heart. Icon: a heart with a worm gnawing it, small pulse line beneath. Label: "WORM 양심의 벌레". Caption words: "Conscience gnaws while sin lives".

Below the figure, a small horizontal strip showing a cliff edge with a downward arrow labeled "FALL TO RUIN 추락", indicating the precipitous path.

Style: flat vector infographic panels with thin outlines and soft cream card backgrounds, engraved-style central figure, consistent sepia-brown and dusty-red accent colors, generous white space, all labels short and legible in a clean serif font.
