Educational infographic, landscape 16:9, in the style of a baroque emblem-book diagram with a modern clean layout: parchment-toned background, engraved-line illustrations, muted sepia and crimson accent palette, clear sans-serif labels.

Title at top center: "The Soul as an Abandoned City" with small Korean subtitle "은총 잃은 영혼 = 버려진 도시" (from Cesare Ripa's Iconologia).

Overall layout: two large side-by-side panels connected by mapping arrows, plus a narrow bottom strip. Eye flow: title → left panel (City) → correspondence arrows → right panel (Soul) → bottom strip (Ants).

LEFT PANEL — header "The City 도시": an engraved walled city drawn twice conceptually in two stacked sub-sections.
Sub-section A, "Who Leaves First": a vertical exodus ladder of four small figures walking out the city gate in order, numbered 1-4, labeled: "1. Prince 군주", "2. Courtiers 조신", "3. Nobles 귀족", "4. Common people 백성". Downward arrow beside them labeled "Order of departure".
Sub-section B, "What Falls First": four buildings crumbling in numbered order: "1. Temple 성전", "2. Prince's Palace 궁전", "3. Fortress 성채", "4. Nobles' Mansions 저택", each with cracks and falling stones.

RIGHT PANEL — header "The Soul 영혼": a mirrored composition of a luminous female figure (the soul) fading, with matching stacked sub-sections.
Sub-section A, four departing lights labeled: "1. God 하나님", "2. Angels 천사들", "3. The Righteous 의인들", "4. All people 사람들".
Sub-section B, four dimming inner flames labeled: "1. Faith 믿음", "2. Charity 사랑", "3. Hope 희망", "4. Intellect·Memory·Will 지성·기억·의지".

Between the panels: four horizontal double-headed arrows for each numbered pair in both sub-sections, visually mapping city element to soul element one-to-one; a small central label on the arrows reads "one-to-one allegory 대응".

BOTTOM STRIP — full width, header "The Ants 개미 = Stings of Lust 육욕의 자극": a line of tiny engraved ants marching from left to right, gnawing the city ruins into dust; caption label "Small desires erode the soul-city".

Style notes: numbered badges in crimson circles, consistent 1-4 numbering across all four lists to emphasize identical order, subtle drop shadows, generous whitespace, everything legible at a glance.
