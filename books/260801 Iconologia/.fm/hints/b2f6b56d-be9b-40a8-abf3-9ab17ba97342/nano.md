Educational infographic, title at top: "PESTE — Plague as an Old Woman (Cesare Ripa)" with small Korean subtitle "역병 도상". Landscape 16:9, clean flat design, muted parchment background, Baroque emblem-book flavor with modern flat vector illustration, dark ink outlines, restrained palette of sickly yellow, ash grey, dark storm blue, blood red.

Center panel: a gaunt, frightening old woman with sickly yellow skin and wild loose hair, seated on a pile of sheep and animal hides, wearing a grey robe stained with yellowish vapor wisps, a wreath of dark storm clouds on her head, holding up a whip with blood-stained cords.

Four callout labels with thin arrows pointing to her features, each label is a small card with an icon and a 3-5 word English phrase plus a short Korean gloss:
1. Arrow to her yellow skin: "Yellow skin = sick body" (누런 피부 = 병든 몸).
2. Arrow to the cloud wreath: "Cloud crown = bad air" (구름 화관 = 나쁜 공기).
3. Arrow to the grey robe: "Grey robe = plague sky" (회색 옷 = 역병 하늘색).
4. Arrow to the animal hides beneath her: "Hides = mass death of beasts" (가죽 = 대량 폐사).

Bottom strip panel, separated by a thin rule: the whip motif enlarged, striking three small figures in a row — a child, a woman, a crowned king — all hit equally, with the label "Whip spares no age, sex, rank" (채찍 = 무차별) beneath them.

Typography: bold condensed sans-serif for title, small clean labels, all label text short and legible, no long sentences.
