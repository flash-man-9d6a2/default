An educational infographic explaining Cesare Ripa's allegorical personification of Stubbornness (Pertinacia) from the Iconologia, in the style of a clean modern museum panel with subtle Baroque engraving accents. Landscape 16:9 aspect ratio. Warm parchment background, dark sepia ink linework, one accent color (deep red) for warnings.

Title banner across the top: "PERTINACIA — Anatomy of Stubbornness" with smaller Korean subtitle "고집의 도상학".

Layout: a large central figure panel flanked by three callout panels on the left and one contrast panel on the right; eye flows from the center figure outward to the callouts, then right to the contrast.

CENTER PANEL: an engraving-style standing woman in a long black dress, arms crossed, face rigid. A thick ivy vine spirals up her body from feet to shoulders. On her head sits a heavy square lead cube. Behind her, a crumbling stone wall partly collapsed with ivy on it. Label under figure: "The Stubborn One (고집)".

LEFT CALLOUTS, three boxes with thin leader lines pointing to the figure:
1. Arrow to the black dress. Label: "Black dress = Ignorance (무지)". Small caption: "mother of stubbornness".
2. Arrow to the lead cube on her head. Label: "Lead die = Dull weight (미련함)". Small caption: "heavy, never rolls".
3. Arrow to the ivy. Label: "Ivy = Rooted opinion (뿌리내린 의견)". Small caption: "never loosens its grip".

BOTTOM STRIP under the center figure: a small three-step process sequence in red showing cause and effect: (1) ivy climbs a wall, (2) ivy roots deepen into cracks, (3) the wall collapses with the ivy still attached. Label: "Destroys its own support (자타 공멸)".

RIGHT CONTRAST PANEL, separated by a vertical divider labeled "VS": a small bright engraving of a child holding onto a raised palm branch, glowing softly. Title: "PERSEVERANZA (항심)". Two-line comparison beneath: "Firmness ruled by REASON (이성)" versus, in red beneath the main figure, "Firmness ruled by IGNORANCE (무지)".

All labels short, 3-5 words, serif typeface like an old book caption. Overall mood: scholarly, elegant, high contrast, easily readable.
