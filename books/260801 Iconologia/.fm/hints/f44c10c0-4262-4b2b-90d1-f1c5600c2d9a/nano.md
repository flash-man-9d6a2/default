Educational comparison infographic, clean flat design with a subtle Renaissance emblem-book flavor (parchment beige background, engraving-style line art figures, muted gold and charcoal accents), landscape 16:9.

Title at top center: "Vain vs Honest Pleasure (헛된 쾌락 vs 정직한 쾌락)" with small subtitle "Cesare Ripa, Iconologia".

Two large side-by-side panels divided by a thin vertical ornamental line, eyes flow left panel then right panel.

LEFT PANEL — header banner "PIACERE VANO — Vain Pleasure (헛된 쾌락)" in deep red:
- Central figure: a richly dressed young man in flamboyant ornate clothing, balancing on top of his head a wide cup holding a glowing red heart, heart fully visible to all.
- Small radiating lines from the heart suggesting exposure.
- Callout label with arrow to the cup: "Heart on display"
- Callout label to his clothes: "Vain finery"
- Bottom caption strip in red: "Pleasure outside God exposes the heart"

RIGHT PANEL — header banner "PIACERE ONESTO — Honest Pleasure (정직한 쾌락)" in deep gold:
- Central figure: Venus in a long modest black robe ("Black Venus"), a jeweled golden girdle around her waist, holding a horse bridle in her right hand and a measuring ruler in her left hand, crescent moon and night sky hinted behind her.
- Callout label with arrow to girdle: "Gold girdle = law"
- Callout label to bridle: "Bridle = restraint"
- Callout label to ruler: "Ruler = measure"
- Bottom caption strip in gold: "Even lawful pleasure needs restraint"

Between the two panels, a small centered "VS" medallion.

Footer strip across the bottom with two short contrast phrases: left "Exposed, fleeting" / right "Veiled, measured".

Typography: clear humanist serif for headers, simple sans-serif for labels, all labels short and highly legible, no long sentences.
