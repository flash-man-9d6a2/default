Educational infographic, clean flat design, 16:9 landscape, white background, thin dark-gray outlines, sans-serif labels.

Title at top center: "Symmetric vs Asymmetric Complementary Pairs" with small Korean subtitle "대칭 배색 vs 비대칭 배색".

Layout: two equal side-by-side panels separated by a thin vertical divider, viewer reads left to right. A small color wheel icon sits at the top of each panel showing two dots on opposite sides connected by an arrow, labeled "Complementary hue" in both panels to show that hue relation is identical.

LEFT PANEL, header "Symmetric: equal partners".
Row 1: two swatches of identical size side by side, left swatch filled with sage green #719470 labeled "Sage #719470", right swatch filled with pale pink #E0B3B6 labeled "Pink #E0B3B6".
Row 2: two swatches of identical size, orange #D96629 labeled "Orange #D96629" and teal #0093A5 labeled "Teal #0093A5".
Below each row: two vertical lightness bars of nearly the same height, axis labeled "Lightness", caption "Similar lightness".
Bottom caption: "Tense standoff, no leader".

RIGHT PANEL, header "Asymmetric: clear hierarchy".
Main visual: one large rectangle filled with deep purple #501345 covering about 75% of the panel area, labeled "Dominant #501345"; on top of it a small bright shape (a circle or a stripe) filled with lime yellow #C7D14F labeled "Accent #C7D14F", glowing slightly like a firefly in dark foliage.
Below: two vertical lightness bars with extreme height difference, tall bar in lime yellow labeled "L ≈ 56%", very short bar in deep purple labeled "L ≈ 19%", axis labeled "Lightness", caption "Extreme lightness gap".
Small arrow pointing from the tall bar to the accent shape labeled "Eye goes here first".
Bottom caption: "Leader and supporter, clear roles".

Footer strip across the bottom, small text: "Same hue contrast, different lightness contrast → hierarchy decides area and attention".

Style: minimal, high legibility, no photographic textures, no extra decorative elements, all labels short, actual HEX colors rendered accurately.
